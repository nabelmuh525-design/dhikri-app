# Privacy Policy — ذِكري | DHIKRI

**Last Updated:** November 20, 2026  
**App Name:** ذِكري | DHIKRI  
**Package Name:** `com.dhikri.islamic`  
**Developer:** Dhikri Studio

## 1. Overview
**ذِكري | DHIKRI** is an offline-first Islamic utility application designed to respect your privacy completely. The app contains **no advertisements**, **no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs**, and **no user account or sign-in requirements**.

## 2. Data Collection & Usage
We do **not** collect, store, sell, or share any personal information, contacts, photos, or behavioral tracking data.

- **Local Preferences & Progress:** Your Quran reading progress, bookmarks, Tasbih counter, selected city for prayer times, and theme preferences are stored **locally on your device** (using Android Room and DataStore) and never leave your device.
- **Location / City Selection:** Prayer times and Qibla direction are calculated locally on your device using your chosen city or coordinates. Location data is never transmitted to external servers.
- **Sensors:** The Qibla compass uses your device's built-in magnetometer/accelerometer sensors locally in real time without recording or transmitting sensor data.

## 3. Optional Network Features
Certain optional features connect to public, read-only endpoints only when you actively use them:
- **Quran Audio Recitations & Ayah Playback:** Streams or downloads Surah/Ayah MP3 audio files from official public Quran audio CDNs (`quranicaudio.com`, `mp3quran.net`, `verses.quran.foundation`).
- **In-App Update Check:** Periodically checks the official GitHub Releases page (`github.com/nabelmuh525-design/dhikri-app`) for newer versions of the app.

## 4. Children's Privacy
The app is suitable for all ages (`Everyone`) and does not knowingly collect any data from children or adults.
